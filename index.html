Heart Knows

How they make you feel — meeting by meeting.

You will not remember the second meeting the way you felt leaving the first. Heart Knows is a quiet place to record how someone actually left you feeling, a couple of hours after you saw them, while your heart still knows. Not a rating. Not a score. Your own honest reading, kept where you can trust it later.

Privacy is the architecture, not a setting. There is no account, no sign-in, and no sync. Your journal lives in an encrypted database on your phone alone, and we do not run a server that receives it.
