Heart Knows Support

A private, on-device journal for how people make you feel, meeting by meeting. On your home screen the app appears as Heart Signals.

Contact us

Email support@heartsignals.app and we will reply within two business days.

Please tell us your device model, your iOS or Android version, and what you were doing when the problem happened. Never send us your journal entries — we do not need them, and we would rather not receive them.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device only, in a SQLCipher-encrypted database. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server that receives what you write. We cannot read your journal, and we cannot recover it for you.

I lost my phone. Can you restore my journal?

No. The encryption key never leaves your device, so nobody — including us — can open your journal without it. If you turned on Settings → Backup → Include in my encrypted iPhone backup, restoring that device backup brings the journal back. Otherwise the only copy is an encrypted export you saved yourself.

How do I export or delete everything?

Settings → Backup writes an encrypted export you can store wherever you like. Settings → Erase everything deletes the journal and its encryption key in one step. Both are free and always available.

What is free, and what is paid?

Logging meetings, check-ins, app lock, and export are always free. The free tier keeps a rolling 30-day retrospective window. An Active chapter subscription (monthly or yearly), or a one-time Lifetime keep, unlocks your full timeline, side-by-side comparison, and Patterns.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — seven days of full history, with no card and no payment. Open the paywall or Settings → Active chapter and tap Start free week.

I paid but the features are still locked.

Tap Restore Purchases on the paywall or in Settings → Active chapter. If it still does not unlock, make sure you are signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought it with, then email us.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by the store, not by us. Manage them at Apple subscriptions or Google Play subscriptions. Cancelling does not delete anything: you return to the rolling 30-day window and your journal stays on the device.

Can I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store that took the payment. Use reportaproblem.apple.com for App Store purchases, or Google Play’s order history for Play purchases.

Why does the app ask for Face ID, the microphone, or speech recognition?

Face ID or your passcode is optional and only locks the app itself. The microphone and speech recognition are requested only if you choose voice entry to dictate your own notes. Notifications are local reminders only — there is no remote push.

Do you collect analytics?

Diagnostics are off by default. If you turn on Settings → Privacy → Share diagnostics, crash reports and a few funnel counts may be sent. Nothing you write is ever included. See the privacy policy for the full detail.

Which languages are supported?

English, Japanese, Korean, Traditional Chinese, Vietnamese, Thai, Malay, Indonesian, German, and French.
